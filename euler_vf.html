⚙ TA/IF financing partners
📊 TA/IF summary by financing partner
Amounts are in ₹ crore. Type is the funding program: TA (trade advance) or IF (inventory funding); a partner that offers both has a row for each. Approved is the total of mappings with status Approved, Activated is the total with status Activated, and Total is Approved plus Activated. Dealers mapped counts every dealer mapped to that partner for that program, whatever the status; the All partners row counts each dealer once. In the insights, unique dealers are counted once per program even if several partners approved or activated them.
